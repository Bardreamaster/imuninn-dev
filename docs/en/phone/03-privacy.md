# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

iMuninn ("the app") streams your iPhone's camera, IMU, ARKit pose and LiDAR depth to your own computer over the **local network**. The app does not collect any personal data.

## Data we do not collect

- The app has no accounts and no servers, and it does not connect to the internet.
- It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
- The developer receives none of your data.

## Permissions and why they are used

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Captures video and streams it to a computer you connect on the same local network |
| Motion & Fitness (accelerometer / gyroscope) | Captures IMU data, sent alongside the video |
| Local Network | Lets your computer discover and connect to the phone via Bonjour (`_imuninn._tcp`) |

Data travels directly between your phone and the computer you connect, with no third party in between. What happens to it on your computer is up to you.

## Data stored on the phone

- Settings (such as the interface language) and configuration presets are stored on the device and removed when you delete the app.
- Local recordings stay in the app's own sandbox on the device, are never uploaded, and are removed when you delete the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.
